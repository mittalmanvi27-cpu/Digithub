<p>Google Ads can be the fastest way to get enquiries — or the fastest way to burn money. The difference is rarely the budget. It's the settings. Here are nine fixes we apply to almost every small-business account.</p>

<h2 id="tracking">1. Track conversions before anything else</h2>
<p>If Google doesn't know which clicks became calls, form fills or WhatsApp chats, it can't optimise — and neither can you. Set up conversion tracking for:</p>
<ul>
  <li>Form submissions (thank-you page or GA4 event)</li>
  <li>Calls from ads (call assets) and clicks on your phone number</li>
  <li>WhatsApp button clicks</li>
</ul>
<div class="callout"><b>Rule of thumb</b>No conversion tracking = no optimisation. Fix this before increasing spend by a single rupee.</div>

<h2 id="search-terms">2–3. Check search terms and add negatives</h2>
<p><strong>2. Read the search terms report weekly.</strong> It shows the actual searches that triggered your ads. You'll often find "free", "jobs", "course", "DIY" or competitor names eating budget.</p>
<p><strong>3. Build a negative keyword list</strong> from what you find and apply it to all campaigns. Start with: <code>free, jobs, career, salary, course, training, pdf, diy, second hand</code> — adjusted to your business.</p>

<h2 id="targeting">4–6. Fix targeting settings</h2>
<p><strong>4. Location: choose "Presence".</strong> The default can show ads to people merely <em>interested</em> in your area. For local services, target people who are actually in or regularly in your location.</p>
<p><strong>5. Turn off the Display Network in Search campaigns.</strong> Leaving it on sends budget to website and app placements that rarely convert for service businesses.</p>
<p><strong>6. Schedule ads for when you can answer.</strong> If calls go unanswered after 8 pm, reduce bids or pause ads then.</p>

<h2 id="pages">7–8. Better ads and landing pages</h2>
<p><strong>7. Use all relevant assets</strong> — sitelinks, callouts, call and location assets. They make ads larger and more clickable at no extra cost per click.</p>
<p><strong>8. Send traffic to a focused landing page,</strong> not your homepage. One service, one clear offer, one form or WhatsApp button, fast on mobile. A better page can lower your cost per lead without touching the ads.</p>

<h2 id="bidding">9. Choose the right bidding strategy</h2>
<table>
  <tr><th>Stage</th><th>Suggested approach</th></tr>
  <tr><td>New account, little data</td><td>Manual CPC or Maximise Clicks with a bid cap, tightly themed keywords</td></tr>
  <tr><td>Steady conversions each month</td><td>Maximise Conversions, then Target CPA once results are stable</td></tr>
  <tr><td>Ecommerce with sales values</td><td>Maximise Conversion Value / Target ROAS</td></tr>
</table>
<p>Smart bidding works best with enough conversion data — switching too early often means Google is guessing with your money.</p>

<blockquote>The best Google Ads accounts aren't the biggest. They're the ones where every rupee is pointed at a buyer who's ready to act.</blockquote>
<p>Work through these nine fixes and review the account weekly. Most businesses see a clear drop in wasted spend within the first month.</p>
