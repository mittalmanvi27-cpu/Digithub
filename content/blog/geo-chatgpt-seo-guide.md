<p>Ask ChatGPT for "the best physiotherapist in Andheri" or "a reliable CA firm for startups in Pune" and you'll get a short list of names — not ten blue links. If your business isn't on that list, you're invisible to a growing share of buyers who never open Google at all.</p>
<p>This guide explains how AI assistants decide who to mention, and what you can do about it this month.</p>

<h2 id="what-changed">What changed</h2>
<p>Search used to be a list of pages. AI assistants now <strong>read those pages for the buyer and write one answer</strong>. Google does the same with AI Overviews at the top of many results. The click that used to land on your website often never happens — the decision is made inside the answer.</p>
<p>That means the goal shifts from "rank on page one" to <strong>"be named and cited inside the answer"</strong>.</p>

<h2 id="seo-aeo-geo">SEO vs AEO vs GEO</h2>
<table>
  <tr><th>Term</th><th>Goal</th><th>Where it shows</th></tr>
  <tr><td><strong>SEO</strong></td><td>Rank your pages in search results</td><td>Google &amp; Bing results, Maps</td></tr>
  <tr><td><strong>AEO</strong> — Answer Engine Optimisation</td><td>Structure content so engines lift your answer directly</td><td>Featured snippets, AI Overviews, voice</td></tr>
  <tr><td><strong>GEO</strong> — Generative Engine Optimisation</td><td>Become a trusted entity that AI models name and cite</td><td>ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot</td></tr>
</table>
<p>They aren't competitors. Good GEO sits on top of good SEO: AI tools that browse the web still rely heavily on pages that search engines already trust.</p>

<h2 id="how-ai-chooses">How AI chooses who to recommend</h2>
<p>No AI company publishes an exact formula, but the patterns are consistent across tools:</p>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Consistent entity information.</strong> Your name, category, location and services should match everywhere — website, Google Business Profile, directories, social profiles.</li>
  <li><strong>Third-party mentions.</strong> AI leans on what <em>others</em> say: reviews, local news, industry lists, association pages, comparison articles.</li>
  <li><strong>Reviews with detail.</strong> "Great service" helps less than "They fixed my knee pain in four sessions and explained every exercise."</li>
  <li><strong>Clear, answer-first pages.</strong> Pages that state plainly what you do, for whom, where, and at what price range are easy to quote.</li>
  <li><strong>Crawlable, structured sites.</strong> Fast pages, clean HTML and schema markup make your facts easy to extract.</li>
</ul>

<blockquote>AI doesn't recommend the business that shouts loudest. It recommends the one that's described most clearly and consistently by the most trustworthy sources.</blockquote>

<h2 id="checklist">The 7-step GEO checklist</h2>
<ol>
  <li><strong>Fix your entity basics.</strong> Use the exact same business name, address, phone and category on your site, Google Business Profile, Justdial, IndiaMART, LinkedIn and any industry directories.</li>
  <li><strong>Write an "About" page an AI can quote.</strong> One paragraph: who you are, what you do, where, since when, for whom, and what makes you different. Avoid vague taglines.</li>
  <li><strong>Create answer-first service pages.</strong> Start each page with a two-sentence answer to the main question ("How much does a 3BHK interior cost in Pune?"), then go deeper.</li>
  <li><strong>Add an FAQ to key pages</strong> using the real questions customers ask on calls and WhatsApp — and mark them up with FAQ schema.</li>
  <li><strong>Add structured data.</strong> At minimum <code>Organization</code> or <code>LocalBusiness</code>, plus <code>Service</code>, <code>FAQPage</code> and <code>Review</code> where relevant.</li>
  <li><strong>Earn mentions on trusted sites.</strong> Local press, "best of" lists, supplier and partner pages, podcasts, association directories. One genuine mention on a respected site beats twenty spammy links.</li>
  <li><strong>Grow detailed reviews.</strong> Ask happy customers to mention the service, location and result. Reply to every review — your replies are content too.</li>
</ol>

<div class="callout"><b>Quick win</b>Ask ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity "Who are the best [your service] in [your city]?" today. Save the answers. Repeat monthly — that's your baseline.</div>

<h2 id="measure">How to measure AI visibility</h2>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Prompt tracking:</strong> keep a list of 15–20 buyer questions and check monthly whether you're named, and which sources are cited.</li>
  <li><strong>Referral traffic:</strong> in GA4, look for sessions from <code>chatgpt.com</code>, <code>perplexity.ai</code>, <code>gemini.google.com</code> and <code>copilot.microsoft.com</code>.</li>
  <li><strong>Branded search:</strong> people who hear your name from AI often Google you next. Rising branded searches in Search Console are a strong signal.</li>
  <li><strong>Lead source question:</strong> add "How did you hear about us?" with an "AI assistant (ChatGPT etc.)" option to your enquiry form.</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="mistakes">Common mistakes</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Publishing dozens of thin, AI-written blog posts. They rarely get cited and can dilute your site.</li>
  <li>Inconsistent business names ("Digitroot", "Digit Root Solutions", "DigiRoot Pvt Ltd") across listings.</li>
  <li>Hiding prices and specifics. AI can't recommend what it can't describe.</li>
  <li>Ignoring Google Business Profile — it feeds Google's AI answers directly for local queries.</li>
</ul>
<p>GEO isn't a trick. It's the discipline of making your business easy to understand and easy to trust — for people and machines alike.</p>
