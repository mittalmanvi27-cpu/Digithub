<p>When someone searches "dentist near me" or "AC repair in Noida", Google shows a map with three businesses before any website. That's the <strong>Maps pack</strong> — and for most local businesses it brings more calls than everything else combined.</p>

<h2 id="why">Why it matters</h2>
<p>Your Google Business Profile (GBP) is free, takes an afternoon to optimise, and also feeds Google's AI Overviews for local questions. Yet most profiles we audit are missing half of what Google asks for.</p>

<h2 id="basics">Fixes 1–5: the basics</h2>
<ol>
  <li><strong>Choose the most specific primary category.</strong> "Pediatric dentist" beats "Dentist" if that's what you do. This is the single most important setting on your profile.</li>
  <li><strong>Add relevant secondary categories</strong> — only ones that genuinely describe services you offer.</li>
  <li><strong>Use your real business name.</strong> Don't stuff keywords ("Best Dentist Noida Smile Clinic"). It breaks Google's guidelines and can get your profile suspended.</li>
  <li><strong>Match your name, address and phone (NAP)</strong> exactly to your website footer and major directories.</li>
  <li><strong>Set accurate hours, including holiday hours.</strong> Being shown as "open" when you're closed costs trust and reviews.</li>
</ol>

<h2 id="content">Fixes 6–10: content</h2>
<ol start="6">
  <li><strong>Write a 750-character description</strong> that says what you do, who for, and where — naturally, in plain language.</li>
  <li><strong>List every service</strong> with a short description and, where possible, a starting price.</li>
  <li><strong>Upload real photos</strong> — storefront, team, work, before/after. Add new ones monthly. Stock images don't help.</li>
  <li><strong>Post updates</strong> every week or two: offers, new work, tips, events. Posts keep your profile active and give searchers a reason to choose you.</li>
  <li><strong>Link to the right page</strong> on your website and add UTM tags (e.g. <code>?utm_source=google&amp;utm_medium=organic&amp;utm_campaign=gbp</code>) so you can measure GBP traffic in GA4.</li>
</ol>

<div class="callout"><b>Pro tip</b>Link your GBP to a location-specific landing page rather than your homepage if you have multiple branches or services.</div>

<h2 id="reviews">Fixes 11–15: reviews &amp; trust</h2>
<ol start="11">
  <li><strong>Create a short review link</strong> from your GBP dashboard and share it on WhatsApp right after a job is done.</li>
  <li><strong>Ask for detail.</strong> "Could you mention the service and how it went?" leads to keyword-rich, persuasive reviews.</li>
  <li><strong>Reply to every review</strong> within 48 hours — thank happy customers, and handle negative ones calmly with a way to resolve offline.</li>
  <li><strong>Never buy or gate reviews.</strong> Fake reviews get filtered and can trigger penalties.</li>
  <li><strong>Get listed consistently</strong> on Justdial, Sulekha, IndiaMART, Practo (for clinics) or your industry's main directory with identical details.</li>
</ol>

<h2 id="factors">What actually ranks you</h2>
<p>Google says local results are based mainly on three things:</p>
<table>
  <tr><th>Factor</th><th>What it means</th><th>What you control</th></tr>
  <tr><td><strong>Relevance</strong></td><td>How well your profile matches the search</td><td>Categories, services, description, website content</td></tr>
  <tr><td><strong>Distance</strong></td><td>How close you are to the searcher</td><td>Accurate address and service areas</td></tr>
  <tr><td><strong>Prominence</strong></td><td>How well known and trusted you are</td><td>Reviews, mentions, links, website authority</td></tr>
</table>
<p>You can't move your shop closer to every customer — but relevance and prominence are fully in your hands. Work through this list, then keep posting, collecting reviews and adding photos every month.</p>
