GEO explained: how to get your business recommended by ChatGPT, Gemini & Perplexity

Buyers now ask AI who to hire. Here's how AI assistants choose which businesses to mention — and a 7-step checklist to make sure yours is one of them.

Digitroot Team22 Sep 20268 min read
✦AI Search

Ask ChatGPT for "the best physiotherapist in Andheri" or "a reliable CA firm for startups in Pune" and you'll get a short list of names — not ten blue links. If your business isn't on that list, you're invisible to a growing share of buyers who never open Google at all.

This guide explains how AI assistants decide who to mention, and what you can do about it this month.

What changed

Search used to be a list of pages. AI assistants now read those pages for the buyer and write one answer. Google does the same with AI Overviews at the top of many results. The click that used to land on your website often never happens — the decision is made inside the answer.

That means the goal shifts from "rank on page one" to "be named and cited inside the answer".

SEO vs AEO vs GEO

TermGoalWhere it shows
SEORank your pages in search resultsGoogle & Bing results, Maps
AEO — Answer Engine OptimisationStructure content so engines lift your answer directlyFeatured snippets, AI Overviews, voice
GEO — Generative Engine OptimisationBecome a trusted entity that AI models name and citeChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot

They aren't competitors. Good GEO sits on top of good SEO: AI tools that browse the web still rely heavily on pages that search engines already trust.

How AI chooses who to recommend

No AI company publishes an exact formula, but the patterns are consistent across tools:

  • Consistent entity information. Your name, category, location and services should match everywhere — website, Google Business Profile, directories, social profiles.
  • Third-party mentions. AI leans on what others say: reviews, local news, industry lists, association pages, comparison articles.
  • Reviews with detail. "Great service" helps less than "They fixed my knee pain in four sessions and explained every exercise."
  • Clear, answer-first pages. Pages that state plainly what you do, for whom, where, and at what price range are easy to quote.
  • Crawlable, structured sites. Fast pages, clean HTML and schema markup make your facts easy to extract.
AI doesn't recommend the business that shouts loudest. It recommends the one that's described most clearly and consistently by the most trustworthy sources.

The 7-step GEO checklist

  1. Fix your entity basics. Use the exact same business name, address, phone and category on your site, Google Business Profile, Justdial, IndiaMART, LinkedIn and any industry directories.
  2. Write an "About" page an AI can quote. One paragraph: who you are, what you do, where, since when, for whom, and what makes you different. Avoid vague taglines.
  3. Create answer-first service pages. Start each page with a two-sentence answer to the main question ("How much does a 3BHK interior cost in Pune?"), then go deeper.
  4. Add an FAQ to key pages using the real questions customers ask on calls and WhatsApp — and mark them up with FAQ schema.
  5. Add structured data. At minimum Organization or LocalBusiness, plus Service, FAQPage and Review where relevant.
  6. Earn mentions on trusted sites. Local press, "best of" lists, supplier and partner pages, podcasts, association directories. One genuine mention on a respected site beats twenty spammy links.
  7. Grow detailed reviews. Ask happy customers to mention the service, location and result. Reply to every review — your replies are content too.
Quick winAsk ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity "Who are the best [your service] in [your city]?" today. Save the answers. Repeat monthly — that's your baseline.

How to measure AI visibility

  • Prompt tracking: keep a list of 15–20 buyer questions and check monthly whether you're named, and which sources are cited.
  • Referral traffic: in GA4, look for sessions from chatgpt.com, perplexity.ai, gemini.google.com and copilot.microsoft.com.
  • Branded search: people who hear your name from AI often Google you next. Rising branded searches in Search Console are a strong signal.
  • Lead source question: add "How did you hear about us?" with an "AI assistant (ChatGPT etc.)" option to your enquiry form.

Common mistakes

  • Publishing dozens of thin, AI-written blog posts. They rarely get cited and can dilute your site.
  • Inconsistent business names ("Digitroot", "Digit Root Solutions", "DigiRoot Pvt Ltd") across listings.
  • Hiding prices and specifics. AI can't recommend what it can't describe.
  • Ignoring Google Business Profile — it feeds Google's AI answers directly for local queries.

GEO isn't a trick. It's the discipline of making your business easy to understand and easy to trust — for people and machines alike.
