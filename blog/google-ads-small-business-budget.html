Google Ads for small businesses: 9 ways to stop wasting your budget

Most small-business ad accounts we audit leak a large share of spend on the wrong searches, places and times. Here's how to plug the leaks.

Digitroot Team28 Aug 20266 min read
↗Google Ads

Google Ads can be the fastest way to get enquiries — or the fastest way to burn money. The difference is rarely the budget. It's the settings. Here are nine fixes we apply to almost every small-business account.

1. Track conversions before anything else

If Google doesn't know which clicks became calls, form fills or WhatsApp chats, it can't optimise — and neither can you. Set up conversion tracking for:

  • Form submissions (thank-you page or GA4 event)
  • Calls from ads (call assets) and clicks on your phone number
  • WhatsApp button clicks
Rule of thumbNo conversion tracking = no optimisation. Fix this before increasing spend by a single rupee.

2–3. Check search terms and add negatives

2. Read the search terms report weekly. It shows the actual searches that triggered your ads. You'll often find "free", "jobs", "course", "DIY" or competitor names eating budget.

3. Build a negative keyword list from what you find and apply it to all campaigns. Start with: free, jobs, career, salary, course, training, pdf, diy, second hand — adjusted to your business.

4–6. Fix targeting settings

4. Location: choose "Presence". The default can show ads to people merely interested in your area. For local services, target people who are actually in or regularly in your location.

5. Turn off the Display Network in Search campaigns. Leaving it on sends budget to website and app placements that rarely convert for service businesses.

6. Schedule ads for when you can answer. If calls go unanswered after 8 pm, reduce bids or pause ads then.

7–8. Better ads and landing pages

7. Use all relevant assets — sitelinks, callouts, call and location assets. They make ads larger and more clickable at no extra cost per click.

8. Send traffic to a focused landing page, not your homepage. One service, one clear offer, one form or WhatsApp button, fast on mobile. A better page can lower your cost per lead without touching the ads.

9. Choose the right bidding strategy

StageSuggested approach
New account, little dataManual CPC or Maximise Clicks with a bid cap, tightly themed keywords
Steady conversions each monthMaximise Conversions, then Target CPA once results are stable
Ecommerce with sales valuesMaximise Conversion Value / Target ROAS

Smart bidding works best with enough conversion data — switching too early often means Google is guessing with your money.

The best Google Ads accounts aren't the biggest. They're the ones where every rupee is pointed at a buyer who's ready to act.

Work through these nine fixes and review the account weekly. Most businesses see a clear drop in wasted spend within the first month.
