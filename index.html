08 — FAQ

Questions, answered.

Still curious? or talk to a human.

How long does SEO take to show results?

Quick technical and Google Business Profile fixes can help within weeks. Meaningful ranking and traffic growth usually takes 3–6 months, depending on your competition and starting point. You'll see the trend every month.

What is AI search optimisation (AEO / GEO)?

It's the work of making your business the source that ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews cite and recommend — through structured, answer-first content, schema, entity signals, reviews and authoritative mentions. It's included in our Standard and Premium SEO plans.

Is the free audit really free?

Yes. We review your website, Google listing, ads and AI visibility, then send a short report with the top issues and quick wins. There's no obligation to work with us.

Do I have to sign a long contract?

No. SEO and PPC plans run month-to-month. We recommend giving SEO at least three months to judge results fairly, but you can stop with 30 days' notice.

Is ad spend included in PPC pricing?

No — our fee covers strategy, setup and management. Your ad budget is paid directly to Google or Meta from your own account, so you stay in full control.

Can you work on my existing website?

Absolutely. Most SEO clients keep their current site. If it's holding you back on speed, mobile or structure, we'll tell you honestly and quote for a fix or a rebuild.
